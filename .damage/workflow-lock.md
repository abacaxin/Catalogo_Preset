# Workflow Lock

- Preserve the approved MVP scope in `docs/02-requirements.md` and `docs/11-master-prompt.md`.
- Do not introduce checkout, payments, WhatsApp Business API, order confirmation, delivery automation, multiple admin roles, or multi-tenant behavior without approved change management.
- Treat WhatsApp handoff as an intent only; persist only its date and time.
- Do not choose production infrastructure, database, authentication, or paid third-party services without technical approval.
- Require explicit approval before production deployment or destructive data operations.
